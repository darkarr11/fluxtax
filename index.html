Our labor should not be taxed. Our natural resources should.

The Flux Tax replaces income, property, and sales taxes with one transparent tax on new goods and services. The rate rises and falls with what government actually needs, and every dollar is out in the open.

THE IDEA IN SHORT

Stop taxing what people earn. Stop taxing what people own. Tax only what people choose to buy new.

No income tax

Your paycheck is yours. No withholding, no filing, no audits, and no one going through your finances every April.

No property tax

When you pay off your home, you own it. You stop paying rent to the government on something you already bought.

A tax on new things only

The tax applies to new goods and services. Used and secondhand goods are never taxed. You decide what you buy, so you decide what you pay. That is the voluntary part.

Natural resources pay their share

Companies that take resources out of the ground pay for what they take, and that money goes to the public that owns them.

The flux

If the government collects more than it needs, the rate goes down for the next cycle. If it comes up short, the rate goes up. The rate follows real need, not politics.

Every dollar visible

Every dollar collected and every dollar spent is published where anyone can see it. People can see exactly what their money paid for.

Federal taxes become a choice

People who want to support federal programs directly can still pay in, with the same transparency and the same surplus refunds.

THE BOTTOM LINE

Your labor belongs to you. Your home belongs to you. Government earns its money by showing you where every dollar goes.
